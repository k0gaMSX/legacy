Another World for MSX
Build 001 - 16th November 2025

Requirements: MSX TurboR, V9990 compatible (e.g. GFX9000 or TRH9000), and OPL4 compatible.
Mapper: ASCII16

MSX TurboR port created by Edd Biddulph a.k.a. cave_fish a.k.a. fizzer.
https://fizzer.neocities.org/

Original game by Eric Chahi, music and sound effects by Jean-Francois Feitas.
Based on the MS-DOS port by Daniel Morais and the reverse-engineering
work of Gregory Montoir.

Known Issues:

 * The game will not run unless an OPL4 device with full YMF278B is detected.
 * Sometimes a harsh glitch noise will occur when transitioning between parts of the game.
 * There are some rendering errors on some polygons.
